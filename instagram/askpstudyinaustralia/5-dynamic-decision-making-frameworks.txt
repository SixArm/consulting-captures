5 DYNAMIC DECISION-MAKING FRAMEWORKS
(When & How to Use Them)

1. OODA Loop
– A fast decision cycle: Observe, Orient, Decide, Act.

2. Cynefin Framework
– Match your decision-making to the problem’s complexity.

3. Five Whys Technique – Ask “Why?” repeatedly to uncover root causes.

4. Chainlink Framework
– Strengthen weak links to improve overall strategy

5. Feedforward (Instead of Feedback)
– Focus on future improvements instead of past mistakes.
